Privacy Policy – Cybersecurity Exam Prep

App: Cybersecurity Exam Prep (package com.s0ulsh3ll.cybersecurityexamprep) · Developer: S0ulSh3ll (Rishabh Dahiya) · Effective: 5 November 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your study progress stays on your device. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK (AdMob) collects to show ads. You can control that through the consent options described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your phone or tablet. It is never uploaded to the developer or any server:

  • Your study progress: answers, mock exam results, streaks, daily goals, mistakes and saved (bookmarked) questions.
  • Your settings: theme, font size and study-reminder time.
  • A random install identifier that the app uses locally to unlock its built-in question bank. It is not linked to you and never leaves the device.

Cloud backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset Study Progress, permanently deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the following with Google:

DataPurpose
Device and advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, App Set ID)Advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from your IP addressAdvertising, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (for example ad views and taps)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics and crash data from the ads SDKApp performance, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. To learn how Google uses data from partner apps, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – used only to load ads. All questions, explanations and mock exams work offline.
  • Notifications – used only for the optional daily study reminder, which is scheduled locally on your device.
  • Run at startup / wake lock – used to reschedule your study reminder after the device restarts.
  • Advertising ID – used by the AdMob SDK as described above.

4. Children

The app is a study tool for adults preparing for professional IT security certifications. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. The question bank is encrypted inside the app. Network traffic from the ads SDK uses HTTPS.

6. Your rights

The developer does not hold any of your personal data, so there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control (see section 1). For data processed by Google for ads, use the controls in section 2 or Google's own privacy tools.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
